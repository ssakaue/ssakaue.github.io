## Publication Titles

- For files under `content/publication/`, use sentence case for `title`.
- Capitalize the first word and the first word after a colon.
- Keep proper nouns capitalized, including person names, organization names, venue names, product names, library/framework names, and acronyms/initialisms.
- Keep capitalization that is standard in technical notation or established terminology when lowercasing would be unnatural or incorrect.
